# Trayport web design direction

## Visual thesis

Confident energy-market infrastructure: deep navy editorial canvases, generous white
space, precise typography, documentary market imagery, and a controlled Trayport orange
accent. The site should feel established and technically exact without inheriting the
legacy theme's accumulated visual noise.

## Content plan

The homepage establishes the brand and proposition with one image-led hero, then proves
the proposition through product, market-coverage, and insight sections before a single
contact call to action.

Interior marketing pages retain the same sequence at a smaller scale:

1. Page identity and primary proposition
2. Supporting context or proof
3. Structured product, market, or editorial detail
4. Related content where it genuinely helps navigation
5. One final action

Article and listing pages prioritise reading and discovery rather than reproducing the
marketing-page composition.

## Interaction thesis

- The global navigation uses a fast, deliberate reveal with clear keyboard focus and no
  ornamental animation.
- Hero media and selected section imagery use subtle scroll-linked depth, disabled for
  reduced-motion users.
- Links, calls to action, and related entities use a consistent underline/arrow movement
  that clarifies affordance without turning the interface into a collection of cards.

Motion must preserve reading order, remain smooth on mobile, and never be required to
understand the content.
